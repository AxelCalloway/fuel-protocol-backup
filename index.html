Glossary & risks

Rank — global mint counter; earlier ranks earn more when claimed later.

Amplifier (AMP) — reward multiplier locked at mint time; declines as the protocol ages.

EAA — Early Adopter Amplification rate locked at mint; boosts early minters.

Term — days until maturity. Claiming within ~1 day of unlock is penalty-free; waiting longer applies a growing penalty.

Risk note: Smart contracts, RPC endpoints, and markets can fail. Fees and rewards are on-chain and can change. Only use funds you can afford to lock or lose. This UI is not financial advice.
