How rewards work
There is no fixed APR. Rewards come entirely from penalties paid by people who unstake early or late. Those penalties are folded back into the pool and split pro-rata across everyone currently staked. Staking longer does not earn more by itself — it just keeps you in the pool while other people’s penalties land.

Grace period after unlock: 14 days before late penalties start.
Early exit = bigger cut the less time served. Late exit also applies a growing penalty.

Penalty mechanics (this contract)
Early exit: penalty = (principal + earned) × unservedDays / totalDuration.
Late exit: after a 14-day grace period past unlock, penalty grows as (time past grace) / 700 days.
100% of every penalty is recycled into the reward pool for remaining stakers — no split, no buy-and-burn routing on this Robinhood deployment.
